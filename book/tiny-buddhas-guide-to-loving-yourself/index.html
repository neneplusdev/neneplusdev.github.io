Tiny Buddha's Guide to Loving Yourself

Lori Deschene

11:13 hours 9 June 2025 - 12 June 2025 Favorite

My Notes

1

Release shame about your past and the limiting beliefs that keep you stuck.

2

See yourself as beautiful and valuable, with all your flaws and weaknesses.

3

Accept yourself more and judge yourself less.

4

Forgive yourself for your mistakes and stop being hard on yourself.

5

Minimize the need for approval to feel more confident.

6

Let go of the comparisons that keep you feeling inferior.

7

Feel complete so that you no longer look to others to fill a void within yourself.

8

Find the courage to share your authentic self for deeper connections with others.

9

Learn to take care of yourself instead of putting everyone else's needs first.

10

Believe that you're valuable so you can start creating a life you love.

11

Because of your smile, you make life more beautiful.

12

The most important thing in this world is to learn to give out love, and let it come in.

13

It isn't what happens to us that causes us to suffer; it's what we say to ourselves about what happens.

14

Be gentle with yourself.

15

To be beautiful means to be yourself. you don't need to be accepted by others. you need to accept yourself.

16

Just be yourself, love yourself, and accept yourself. that is true beauty.

17

Beautiful doesn't mean being physically attractive or looking like those people we see on tv or in magazines. beautiful means just being and loving you.

18

The moment you accept yourself you become beautiful.

19

What you are inside shows on your face.

20

Doing what you love is an important step in loving who you are.

21

If we learn to open our hearts, anyone, including the people who drive us crazy, can be our teacher.

22

Every person you meet has something special to give you—that is, if you are open to receiving it.

23

Be gentle first with yourself if you wish to be gentle with others.

24

Things and conditions can give you pleasure but they cannot give you joy—joy arises from within.

25

When you lose, don't lose the lesson.

26

Accept where you are. you will immediately suffer less.

27

Your task is not to seek love, but merely to seek and find all the barriers within yourself that you have built against it.

28

What i am is good enough if i would only be it openly.

29

What other people think of me is none of my business.

30

Love isn't supposed to be fearful. love means accepting a person, flaws and all.

31

Love is about mutual respect for each other and fully appreciating a person without trying to change them.

32

Criticism is something you can easily avoid by saying nothing, doing nothing, being nothing.

33

The need for approval kills freedom.

34

It takes courage to grow up and become who you really are.

35

No one in the entire world can do a better job of being you than you.

36

Life is what happens to you while you're busy making other plans.

37

If you make friends with yourself, you will never be alone.

38

Learn to be comfortable being alone.

39

When you learn to enjoy your alone time, you'll never need another person to fill the space.

40

Don't think you're on the right road just because it's a well-beaten path.

41

Our lives only improve when we are willing to take chances—and the first and most difficult risk we can take is to be honest with ourselves.

42

Self-honesty has a way of breaking through walls—big walls.

43

Being honest with ourselves is the surest way to move forward on the path of self-discovery.

44

Have respect for yourself, and patience and compassion. with these, you can handle anything.

45

The way you treat yourself sets the standard for others.

46

You don't have to worry about burning bridges if you're building your own.

47

We have to accept ourselves to be accepted by others, and we have to teach people how to treat us.

48

We deserve to be treated like the amazing, beautiful souls we are.

49

Happiness does not come from pleasing other people. happiness comes from feeling content with your own life and goals.

50

Be kind to yourself and life will be a whole lot brighter.

51

It's not the years in your life that count. it's the life in your years.

52

The grass is always greener where you water it.

53

You're the pilot of your life.

54

Always concentrate on how far you've come, rather than how far you have left to go.
